THEO KITCHEN & BAR — LANDING PAGE
=================================
Open index.html in your browser. To publish: upload the entire folder with index.html, styles.css, script.js and assets/ to your host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Vercel, etc.).

SITE SECTIONS
- Responsive navigation and booking phone link
- Hanging Edison bulbs with pure CSS
- Restaurant exterior, THEO event image, green mood-board images
- Menu cards and clickable full scanned photo menu
- Live music / DJ atmosphere section
- Gallery, maps, phone and social links

IMAGE SOURCES
- assets/theo-exterior.webp and theo-events.webp: crops of your uploaded THEO story screenshots. Some story overlays may be visible.
- assets/theo-*-menu.webp: your uploaded menu screenshots, cropped to the menu page.
- assets/green-*.webp: your uploaded mood-board reference images, not photographs of THEO; confirm your right to publish them or replace with licensed photography.

The website includes direct links to Google Maps and Tripadvisor. To use their photos, obtain the rights and add approved image files to assets/.

FONT
- 'Nothing You Could Do' used as a close freely available handwritten-font match to the supplied menu script. It is not an exact extraction of a proprietary menu typeface.
- Montserrat and DM Sans via Google Fonts.

Static site: booking uses phone calls; no reservation backend is included.
